What is BMI?

A simple overview of Body Mass Index and how it is used.

Body Mass Index, commonly called BMI, is a numerical screening measure calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used to group adults into broad weight categories.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

BMI can be useful as one general screening measure, but it does not directly measure body fat. Factors such as muscle mass, body composition, age, genetics and other aspects of health are not captured by the BMI number.

Why BMI has limitations

A BMI result should be considered alongside other relevant health information rather than used as a complete assessment of health.
